# Limits, provenance and ethical considerations

- The controlled WEST/EAST environment is generated and numerical; its labels derive from the rules constructing observations. It does not test actual atmospheric transport or identify real emitters.
- Only 24 controlled episodes, including 16 reversals. All four message conditions reuse these episodes, and each receiver has a single sampled reply per condition and timepoint.
- Message-content and claimed timestamp were crossed experimentally, but GPT v3/v4 arms were collected in separate sessions; the factorial analysis is exploratory and not a clean contemporaneous randomized replication.
- Gemini and Qwen were **reviewers of GPT-created peer reports**; they were not full autonomous swarms generating peer messages afresh.
- Completion and communication topology were not varied; no agent tools took outside-world actions.
- Some identical control prompts had different outputs attributable to stochastic/provider variation.
- The 360/360 historical-air-quality classification result follows from a prompt providing both current measured PM2.5 and the cutoff; it supplies no evidence that quarantine improves safety.
- The protocol's source-choice labels are not official environmental attribution. The exercise is intended for safer information-handoff evaluation, not enforcement, public-health alerts, or accusations against particular sources.
- The archived results have not been independently externally peer reviewed.
